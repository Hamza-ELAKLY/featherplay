What it does

What a music tab does, in a window that stays out of the way.

  • Links and searchPaste a video, playlist or Mix, or search YouTube; results keep loading as you scroll.
  • A real queueRepeat, shuffle, drag to reorder, play next, and Start radio for a song's Mix.
  • Pins and historyKeep favourite tracks and playlists close; recent plays are one click away.
  • Waveform seek barMeasured from the track itself, so you can see where the quiet parts are.
  • Stays out of the wayPlays from the system tray, with media keys and the Windows media overlay.
  • Picks up where you left offQueue, position and settings are kept between runs.
  • Light, dark or automaticFollows Windows, or the theme you pick.
  • PortableEverything stays in its own folder; delete it and it's gone.

Get Featherplay

Windows 10 and 11

64-bit. One zip with Featherplay and yt-dlp, which the app keeps up to date.

Download the latest release Featherplay-<version>-windows-x64.zip
  1. Download the zipFrom the latest release on GitHub, with its SHA256SUMS.txt to check it against.
  2. Unzip it anywhereYour Documents, a USB stick: it keeps everything in that folder.
  3. Run featherplay.exeTo remove it later, delete the folder.

Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" on first run, because the program isn't code-signed yet (it's under way). Choose More info → Run anyway. See the code signing policy.

Signing in (optional)

Only for the Library tab. Everything else works without an account.

Featherplay asks Google for view-only access to your YouTube account (youtube.readonly) to show your liked videos and playlists, private ones included. What it reads stays on your PC, encrypted for your Windows account; there's no Featherplay server. Sign out in Settings to remove the access and your library's copy. Read the privacy policy.

It reads

Your liked videos, your playlists and their tracks, your channel's name and picture.

It can't

Change, upload, like, comment on or delete anything.

While Google reviews Featherplay, sign-in is open to test accounts only. To be added, email featherplay-support@googlegroups.com with your Google account's address.
