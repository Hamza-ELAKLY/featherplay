A feather-light player
for YouTube audio

Featherplay plays the audio of YouTube videos, playlists and Mixes in a small desktop window. One portable program, about 50 MB of memory while playing, no ads and no telemetry.

Download for Windows Source on GitHub

Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit · nothing to install · free and open source (GPL-3.0)

What it does

  • Links and searchPaste a link to a video, playlist or Mix, or search YouTube; results keep loading as you scroll.
  • A real queueRepeat, shuffle, drag to reorder, play next, and Start radio for a song's Mix.
  • Pins and historyKeep favourite tracks and playlists close; recent plays are one click away.
  • Waveform seek barMeasured from the track itself, so you can see where the quiet parts and the drops are.
  • Smooth playbackPause, resume and track changes fade instead of cutting.
  • Stays out of the wayPlays from the system tray, with media keys and the Windows media overlay.
  • Picks up where you left offQueue, position and settings are kept between runs.
  • Light, dark or automaticFollows Windows, or the theme you pick.
  • Portable and up to dateEverything stays in its own folder. Updates are signed and checked before they're installed, and automatic checks can be turned off.

Signing in with Google (optional)

Everything above works without an account. Signing in adds a Library tab with your liked videos and your playlists, private ones included, ready to play.

To do that, Featherplay asks Google for view-only access to your YouTube account (the youtube.readonly permission). It uses it only to show your liked videos and playlists in the app. It can't change, upload, like, comment or delete anything. What it reads stays on your PC, encrypted for your Windows account: Featherplay has no server, and nothing is shared or sold. Signing out in Settings removes the access and the data.

Read the privacy policy for the details.

While Google reviews Featherplay, sign-in is open to test accounts only. To be added, email featherplay-support@googlegroups.com with your Google account's address.
