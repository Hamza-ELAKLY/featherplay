# Code of conduct

Featherplay follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short: be kind and patient, assume good faith, welcome newcomers, and keep
discussion about the work. Harassment, insults and personal attacks have no place
here, in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere the project is represented.

If someone's behaviour worries you, contact the maintainer privately at
[featherplay-support@googlegroups.com](mailto:featherplay-support@googlegroups.com),
which only the maintainer reads. Reports are kept confidential. The maintainer may remove comments, lock threads, or block people who
don't respect this code.
